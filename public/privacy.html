Privacy Policy for PocketNORCET

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

PocketNORCET ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when you use the PocketNORCET mobile application and web companion.

1. Information We Collect

PocketNORCET does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Account Required: You can use all core study features, mock tests, and revision tools without creating an account or providing your name, email, or phone number.
  • Local Device Storage: Your study progress, bookmarked clinical concepts, and mock test scores are stored strictly locally on your own device using local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not track your location, browsing habits, or personal identifiers.

2. Network Usage

The app only uses network connectivity for:

  • Checking for official app version updates once daily via our public read-only configuration endpoint.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not incorporate third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics trackers, or social media sharing tracking SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

PocketNORCET is designed for nursing students, healthcare professionals, and adult examination candidates preparing for AIIMS NORCET, ESIC, and state nursing officer examinations. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

5. Security

Because all progress data is stored locally in your device's sandbox, your data remains as secure as your device.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us via our official GitHub repository: https://github.com/imsrpanda/NORCET
